# Support

This page provides support information for the iOS app "성남 아동수당".

## App Purpose

The app helps users find Seongnam child allowance merchant locations on a map, filter by category, view merchant details, and open map apps for directions.

## Data Notice

Merchant information is based on public data and geocoding results. Store status, category, address, and payment availability may change. Please confirm with the merchant when necessary.

# Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 12, 2026

This app helps users find Seongnam child allowance merchant locations on a map.

## Data Used by the App

The app uses public merchant information provided through data.go.kr, including merchant name, category, address, postal code, reference date, and geocoded coordinates.

## Location

If the user grants location permission, the app uses the device location to show nearby merchants and center the map. Location data is processed for app functionality and is not stored by the app developer on a separate server.

## Advertising

The app may display ads through Google AdMob. Google AdMob may collect or process device, advertising, usage, diagnostic, and approximate location related data according to Google's advertising and privacy policies.

## Third-Party Services

The app may use these third-party services:

- Kakao Maps SDK for map display
- Google AdMob for ads
- Public GitHub-hosted data files for app data updates

## Public Data Accuracy

Merchant data and geocoding results may differ from actual payment availability. Users should confirm with the merchant or official payment provider before relying on the information.

## Contact

For privacy or support inquiries, contact the app developer through the support page in this repository.
